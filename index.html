Waterly

Drink water on a schedule that fits your day.

What it does

Waterly is an Android app for people who forget to drink water. You tap once for every glass you finish. Waterly counts it against your daily goal and tells you when the next glass is due.

Count glasses
One tap logs a glass (220 ml by default). A quick confirmation stops accidental taps.
Plan the day
On your first drink, tell Waterly when you will sleep. It spreads the remaining glasses across the hours you are awake and re-plans after every glass.
Get reminded
Notifications at each drink time, or a louder alarm that plays an audio file you pick from your phone.
Keep a streak
One tap a day builds a streak.
Use the widget
Log a glass or mark your streak from the home screen without opening the app.
Back up
Optionally save your streak and history to your own Google Drive so a cleared app or a new phone doesn’t reset it.

Your data stays yours

Everything Waterly records lives on your phone. If you connect Google Drive, a backup is written to a hidden folder that only Waterly can open. There is no Waterly account, no server and no advertising. The privacy policy has the details.
